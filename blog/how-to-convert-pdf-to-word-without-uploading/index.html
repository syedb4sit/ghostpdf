1. Reconstructing Headings & Paragraphs Locally Without Risky Cloud Uploads

Receiving a contract, whitepaper, or syllabus strictly as a locked-layout PDF forces tedious manual retyping if you just want to revise two paragraphs in Word or reuse clean formatted copy. Yet uploading signed vendor contracts or tax memos into ad-funded cloud converters breaks client confidentiality rules instantly.

Running PDF.js text geometry extraction paired with the docx generator right inside browser RAM analyzes font sizes and baseline vertical spacing locally—converting body lines, section headers, and structured paragraphs into a native .docx file ready for Microsoft Word, Google Docs, or LibreOffice.

2. Local PDF-to-Editable-DOCX Workflow

  1. Confirm Text Is Selectable: Verify you can highlight text in the PDF; if pages are purely scanned images, route straight to PDF OCR instead.
  2. Load Your PDF Locally: Drop the document into PDF to Word directly in your browser tab.
  3. Let Local Structural Parsing Run: PDF.js groups adjacent text runs by horizontal baseline and font weight, while docx builds native Word paragraphs.
  4. Inspect Complex Tables & Margins in Word: Open the downloaded .docx and tidy complex decorative borders or multi-column boxes before circulating drafts.
