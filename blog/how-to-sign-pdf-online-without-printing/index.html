1. Replacing Print-Sign-Scan Loops With Crisp Vector Signatures in Seconds

Printing a clean crisp PDF offer letter on physical paper, signing it with ballpoint ink, photographing it crookedly on a kitchen table, and scanning a muddy 12 MB bitmap PDF back to HR destroys sharpness—and forces recipients to squint at shadowed margins.

Signing directly on-screen preserves 100% crisp vector contract typography underneath while placing your drawn stylus stroke, clean cursive typed mark, or transparent PNG signature straight onto the exact signature line locally in browser memory.

2. Signing & Finalizing PDF Contracts Without Touching Paper

  1. Load Your Unsigned Agreement: Open Sign PDF directly inside your browser and drop the NDA, lease, or freelancer contract.
  2. Create or Import Your Signature: Draw naturally on trackpad/touchscreen, pick a crisp typed cursive script, or upload a transparent ink PNG cutout.
  3. Position Accurately & Resize to Fit the Line: Switch to the signature page, place and scale your autograph neatly above printed signature rules without covering printed text.
  4. Freeze Form Fields & Signatures Permanently: Download the signed copy, then flatten it via Flatten PDF so interactive layers cannot shift across mobile viewers.

  • Never Leave Signature Images Floating Editable When Returning Final Bids: Pair Sign PDF with Flatten PDF so recipients cannot easily grab or shift overlay objects.
  • Compare Returned Counter-Signed Versions Carefully: Run finalized counter-signed agreements against your outgoing copy in Compare PDF to confirm zero subtle text tampering occurred.
