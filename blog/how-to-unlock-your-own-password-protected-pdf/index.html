1. Eliminating Repetitive Password Prompts From Authorized Bank & Payslip Archives

Financial institutions, telecom carriers, and payroll portals routinely encrypt monthly statements with open passwords (like your tax ID or birth date). While great during email transit, storing thirty separately password-locked PDFs inside local tax folders makes quarterly accounting painful—especially when trying to merge statements, annotate receipts, or print packets.

Most PDF manipulation libraries refuse to merge or organize encrypted documents directly. Once you possess the legitimate opening password, saving a permanently decrypted personal copy locally removes repetitive prompts cleanly so standard merging, numbering, and archive searching work smoothly.
