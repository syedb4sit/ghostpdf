1. Pulling Just Page 7 From a 100-Page Legal or HR Scan Cleanly Without Screenshots

HR departments and closing agents love emailing monolithic 80-page welcome packets, tax PDFs, or multi-tenant master scans when you only actually need to return page 14 (the signed direct deposit authorization) or isolate page 32 (one notarized certificate). Forwarding the entire 80-page dossier leaks unrelated third-party clauses and exceeds portal upload sizes cleanly.

Rather than photographing your screen or printing to a degraded OS print dialog, extracting single exact pages directly via PDF-Lib preserves 100% original resolution and vector clarity into a clean standalone 1-page PDF in seconds.
