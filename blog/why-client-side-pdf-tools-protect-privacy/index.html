1. Cloud Upload Queues vs. In-Browser Memory: What Actually Happens to Your PDF Bytes

When you drop a tax return, signed employment agreement, medical test result, or bank statement onto a conventional online PDF site, step one happens silently before you click any edit button: an HTTP POST transfers your raw binary document across the internet to remote application servers—where cloud load balancers, temporary /tmp directories, analytics logs, and third-party object buckets store copies outside your control.

GhostPDF flips that entire architecture upside down. Every utility runs strictly client-side inside your active browser tab using JavaScript and WebAssembly libraries (PDF-Lib, PDF.js, Tesseract, Mammoth, SheetJS). Static code downloads once cleanly over HTTPS; your private document bytes never traverse the network at all—staying inside local RAM until your download finishes and the tab clears.

2. Auditing Zero-Upload Privacy Yourself in Under 15 Seconds

  1. Open Any GhostPDF Tool Cleanly: Launch Lock PDF, Redact PDF, or Merge PDF in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
  2. Open Browser DevTools (F12 → Network Tab): Watch live network requests while dragging a 10 MB PDF into the drop zone—observe zero outbound upload POST payloads containing your document.
  3. Verify Instant Offline Capability After Load: Disconnect Wi-Fi after a tool loads and watch merging, rotating, redacting, or locking continue functioning smoothly locally!
  4. Combine Privacy Hygiene With Document Hardening: Pair zero-upload execution cleanly with Redact PDF, PDF Metadata, and Lock PDF for defense-in-depth.
