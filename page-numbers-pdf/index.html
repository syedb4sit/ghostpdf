How to Add Page Numbers to a PDF

1. Select Your PDF

Drag and drop any multi-page PDF document above to open Page 1 with live page number preview.

2. Pick Format, Position & Start Number

Choose between "Page X", "Page X of N", or "- X -", pick top or bottom alignment (left, center, right), and set your starting page number.

3. Preview & Download

Inspect every page using "Apply & Preview Whole PDF" or click download to save your numbered PDF locally.
